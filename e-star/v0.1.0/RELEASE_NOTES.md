# E-star v0.1.0 — anonymous unreviewed preprint

This release contains the anonymous manuscript (8 pages) and a portable reviewer/reproduction package. It is prepared for mathematical review and is not a peer-reviewed or priority-certified result.

The bounded-basis window is a direct corollary of Lev (1997), Theorem 1-prime. The combinatorial bridge and good-core framework are prior work; the Lean bridge is an independent reimplementation. The proposed application is the q-1 forbidden subset-sum range and the q=4, c=2/3 congruence-class limit. Literature gaps and AI assistance are explicitly disclosed in the package.

The package was extracted freshly: its manifest and three offline finite-check suites passed. The optional Lean entry point checked fourteen arithmetic/finite-combinatorial declarations, with standard axioms only, and replayed the new distinctification module. This is not an end-to-end formal proof of the manuscript.
